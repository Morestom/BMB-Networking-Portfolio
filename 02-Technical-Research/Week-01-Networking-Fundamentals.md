# Week 01 — Networking Fundamentals

The first week focused on the technologies and protocols that support communication across local and wide-area networks.

## Topics studied

- OSI and TCP/IP models and the responsibilities of their respective layers.
- IPv4 addressing, subnet masks, and subnetting.
- LAN/WAN design, access and trunk ports, and VLANs.
- Static and dynamic routing fundamentals.
- Application-layer services and protocols, including DNS, DHCP, HTTP/HTTPS, SMTP, SSH, and SNMP.

The research established the protocol foundation for the subsequent Packet Tracer configuration exercises.
