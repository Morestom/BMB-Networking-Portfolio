# BMB Networking Internship Portfolio

A portfolio of networking labs and technical research completed during my internship at **BMB**.

My practical work focused on Cisco Packet Tracer simulations, including Layer 2 switching, IP routing, network services, and troubleshooting. I also prepared research reports on enterprise networking and cybersecurity concepts.

The pages in this repository describe the exercises and their technical scope. **Original Packet Tracer assignment files and lab simulations are not distributed here.** Security research is presented as conceptual study, not deployment experience.
